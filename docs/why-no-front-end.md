# Why there is no front end

Underneath Vanta's screens sit ordinary records: frameworks, controls, evidence, policies, people, systems, suppliers and risks, and a handful of jobs you repeat every week. A large part of what you pay for is the layer on top that lets people who do not write SQL get at those records. Screens, filters, dashboards, forms.

That layer used to be the whole product, because talking to a database was hard. It is not hard any more. Open this folder in Claude Code, describe what you want, and it writes the query, runs it, and explains the answer. Ask a question no standard report covers and you still get an answer.

## What you gain

- **Better answers.** A dashboard shows what the vendor decided to chart. Here you ask your own question, in your own words, and get it answered against your own data.
- **No seats, no add-ons.** Everyone who needs to look can look. Questionnaires and supplier reviews are commands, not extra modules.
- **Your data in your Postgres.** Plain tables. Back them up, query them from anything, leave any time. The audit log of every change is itself evidence.
- **A process that matches you.** When your way of working changes, you add a command. You do not wait for a feature request to clear.

## What you give up

- **Hourly automated tests.** Vanta connects to your cloud, identity and code systems and tests them every hour. Here, Claude Code runs read-only checks with your own tools when you ask, and records the result. Enterprise DNA can build scheduled checks into your version.
- **A staff portal.** Vanta gives staff a place to accept policies and install its device agent. Here, a person records acceptance and training dates.
- **A trust centre page.** `npm run docs` renders a security summary you send yourself; Enterprise DNA can publish it as a page.
- **A phone app.** It runs where Claude Code runs.
- **A vendor help desk.** This is open source. Enterprise DNA supports the installed version for businesses that want someone to call.

## Who this fits

Small teams who already use Claude Code, or who would rather learn to ask than learn another interface. If you need hourly automated tests across dozens of systems and a portal for every employee, keep Vanta or have that built. If you need the register, the evidence trail and the answers more than the screens, this is cheaper, faster and yours.
